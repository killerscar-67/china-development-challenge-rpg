# China Development Challenge RPG — Plan

## Experience
A single-player or small-group browser text RPG translating the 35-slide classroom activity into one continuous simulation.

## Core loop
1. Assemble the development team.
2. Diagnose the equal-reward / unequal-effort farming problem.
3. Predict the effect on motivation and production.
4. Spend two reform coins across four reform paths.
5. Solve five development challenges with problem → reform → effect reasoning.
6. Complete a rapid four-question matching round.
7. Write an exit-ticket explanation and review the big picture.

## Verification criteria
- The user can progress from title to results without dead ends.
- Score, progress, decision log, reform choices, reveals, and replay all work.
- The four reforms are represented faithfully: Rural Reform, Private Enterprises, SOE Reform, and Opening-up.
- The generated campaign artwork is used in the title scene.
- The app remains usable at desktop and mobile widths.

## Risk slices
- State-driven phase progression and reset behavior.
- Two-coin reform selection constraint.
- Challenge and final-round reveal logic.
- Responsive layout for dense decision screens.

## Out of scope
- Accounts, database, multiplayer networking, live timer enforcement, and server APIs.
